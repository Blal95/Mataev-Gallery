# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors browsing Bilal R. Mataev's photography: friends, recruiters, and people
arriving from mataev.no. Mostly one-off visits on phones and laptops; they scroll
the feed, open a few frames, maybe check where a shot was taken. A second audience
is developers consuming the public photo API.

## Product Purpose

A standalone photography platform (gallery.mataev.no) that is both a browsable
portfolio and the public photo API behind mataev.no. Success: the photographs feel
like the entire point; chrome stays out of the way; a frame opened on a phone is as
immersive as on a desktop.

## Brand Personality

Archival, precise, warm. A film archive / contact-sheet metaphor carried by a warm
near-black background, film-amber accent, serif italic captions (Playfair) and mono
spec labels (JetBrains). Quiet confidence, no marketing voice.

## Anti-references

- Stock photo-portfolio templates (Squarespace gallery look).
- Cold blue-gray "tech dark mode"; the palette stays warm.
- Social-app chrome: like buttons, avatars, engagement bait.
- Overlays that cover the photograph; the image is never sacrificed for UI.

## Design Principles

1. The photograph wins every layout conflict; UI yields space to the image.
2. Archive grammar: frame numbers, EXIF as instrument readouts, hairline rules.
3. One accent (film amber) does all the pointing; everything else is neutral.
4. Mobile is a first-class viewing room, not a squeezed desktop.
5. Motion is brief and exponential-out; reduced-motion users get instant states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigation throughout (arrows, Escape, I, shortcuts overlay), visible
focus rings, focus trap in the modal, reduced-motion alternatives for every
animation, 44px touch targets on mobile, alt text from captions.
